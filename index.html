Snow Covers the 'berg – Prepare for the Cold

A picture of snow on the Drakensberg

Severe weather is expected as a strong cold front moves across parts of South Africa. The cold front is expected to bring very low temperatures, strong winds and possible snowfall in high-lying areas such as the Drakensberg. Residents are advised to prepare for colder conditions by keeping warm and avoiding unnecessary travel in areas where roads may become slippery or difficult to use. The weather may continue to remain cold throughout the weekend, with some areas experiencing rain and cloudy conditions. People living in mountainous areas should take extra care as temperatures may drop below freezing during the night. Motorists are also encouraged to drive carefully, especially in places where snow or ice may affect road conditions.
